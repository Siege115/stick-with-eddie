**Fact check against the sources**
- Wilhelm von Osten, a Berlin maths teacher, showed Hans around 1900–1904 and charged no admission. ✔
- The New York Times wrote about Hans in 1904 ("Berlin's Wonderful Horse", 4 Sept 1904). The newspaper in the video is invented (no real masthead) and only quotes the date. ✔
- A 13-person commission found no trick in September 1904. ✔
- In 1907 Oskar Pfungst showed Hans was right ~89% of the time when he could see the questioner and ~6% when he couldn't (narrated as "almost everything wrong"). ✔
- Hans read small, unconscious changes in posture and face as the count reached the answer. ✔
