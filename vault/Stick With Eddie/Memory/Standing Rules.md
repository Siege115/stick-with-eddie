# Standing Rules (read first)

**Owner control**
- The owner sets **every posting date and the cadence**. Never propose or change dates unless asked.
- The owner picks each **topic** before scripting. If the owner says "you pick", choose using the bible, explain each pick in one line, and record it in [[Decisions]].
- **Nothing public or paid without an explicit yes** for that exact action: upload, schedule, publish, reply to comments, post anywhere, paid generations (ElevenLabs, Higgsfield, vidIQ credits).
- **Pictures before video:** the owner reviews still boards before any full render.

**Channel format** (see [[Channel Format]])
- Shorts only, 9:16, **55–65 s** (up to about 68 s is fine when needed for clarity; the owner prefers unrushed over short). True fun-fact stories: hook → "You see…" → conflict → rule of three → payoff in the last line. Ends cold (no CTA). The last frame loops into the first.
- Facts must be true and sourced (fact-checker APPROVED). Sources go in the description. Kid-safe. Borrow format from Ricks Clips, never copy.
- Captions: small, 1–2 words, lower-middle, never over faces, numbers in green.

**Look and characters**
- Flat cut-outs (pill bodies, flat white faces, thick outlines) inside detailed 3D sets. Rich background detail, readable **English** props for this audience (local language only where English would be implausible, e.g. French signs in Martinique 1806).
- **Eddie always keeps his sneakers** in every costume. Eddie's egg-shaped head is Eddie-only; nobody else may read as Eddie.
- **Log cameo:** a one-frame running easter egg in every Short, seen *through* a window or porthole (behind glass, inside a room). Our own drawing.
- Smooth single silhouettes (no bumps from overlapping shapes); no clipping; people walk *behind* things; props exist before people interact with them.
- Period-correct: no bowlers before 1849, no gas lamps in 1806, no photos in 1806 papers, sail only before steam, no Suez before 1869.
- Respectful depiction of every culture; no caricature.

**QA and owner lessons (S02 v1)**
- Physical sense: slide on ice, visible ropes and pulleys, hats held in hands, no floating props; birds fly their own paths.
- Hold key actions 2.5 s+; no dead air before reveals (keep ambience under pauses).
- **Proof shots:** when the narration claims something ("most of the ice is still there", "he tried hay"), the object must be clearly on screen.
- No near-black frames in pushes through doors; no floating props or hats; dialogue lines are speech bubbles only (no duplicate captions); captions stay 1–2 words.
- Never reuse another Short's voice lines by accident (S01's "Wunderbar!" leaked into S02 cheers).
- No transcription tool is available here, so a person should listen once for swallowed words.

**Sound**
- Music is warm and acoustic-sounding (pizzicato, soft piano, bass, light percussion). **Never 8-bit or chiptune.** Notes fade out; no clicks.
- −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. One clop or effect per counted action.
