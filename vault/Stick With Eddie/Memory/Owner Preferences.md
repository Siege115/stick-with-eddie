# Owner Preferences

- Wants a **professional team feel**: the manager plus 12 specialists ([[youtube-manager]]).
- Reviews **pictures first**, then the video. Gives feedback in short messages and expects every point fixed.
- Notices small visual details: bumps in shapes, hair not sitting on heads, clipping, things appearing from nowhere, spelling (prefers English words like MONDAY and FREE ADMISSION), scale (faces too big or too small), dark shots.
- Likes **rich detail**: plants, accessories, props, backgrounds.
- Sometimes delegates choices ("you pick"). Decide, explain briefly, record.
- Likes fun easter eggs (the Tung Tung Tung Sahur–style log cameo).
- Wants knowledge saved in Obsidian: this Memory folder, [[Home]], and `Stick With Eddie Mainpage.base`.
- Supplied an editing guide library: `reference/editing/` (hooks, framing, colour, sound, comedy). Use it.
- Wants everything committed and pushed to GitHub: **github.com/Siege115/stick-with-eddie** (main).
