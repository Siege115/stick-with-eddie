# Analytics Routine
Check each Short at **48 hours** and **7 days**: views, % stayed to watch, average view, retention drop points (with what is on screen), traffic sources, subscribers. Turn each finding into one change for the next Short. Owner: [[analytics-analyst]]. Connect @stickwitheddie to vidIQ for live data.
