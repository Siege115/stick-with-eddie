# S02 Boston Crowd

**Role:** Boston wharf crowd, 1806 (laughs) and 1833 (cheers)

**Look:** 1806: tall top hats, a bicorne for an older man, round hats, knit caps, knee breeches and white stockings, empire-waist gowns with sashes, spencer jackets, shawls, bonnets. 1833: frock coats, natural-waist dresses with puffed (leg-of-mutton) sleeves, bonnets. No bowlers.

**Details:** The same people who laugh in 1806 cheer in 1833 (that reversal is the beat). A child appears on the wharf only, never on the ice. Poses: `laughPoint`, `laughBelly`, `laughBack`, `kneeSlap`, `cheer`, `clap`. Costume ids: b06*, b33*.
