**Role:** Host / hero of every story

**Look:** Bald black-and-white stickman, egg-shaped head, tapered limbs, sneakers

**Details:** Plays the hero in costume. S01: von Osten (long dark coat, wide-brimmed hat with band and buckle, grey beard with strands, chalk).
