# S02 · The Man Who Shipped Ice to the Tropics ("The Ice King")

**Chosen by the owner on 2026-11-17.** The true story of **Frederic Tudor** of Boston, who shipped winter pond ice to hot countries.

## Draft story shape (not final until fact-checked and scripted)
- **Hook:** *He filled a ship with ice from a frozen pond and sailed it to the Caribbean.*
- **You see…** In 1806 there were no fridges, and in hot countries nobody had a cold drink. Tudor thought he could sell them winter.
- **Conflict:** the Boston Gazette mocks him in print: *"No joke… We hope this will not prove a slippery speculation."*
- **Rule of three:** ① Martinique: nobody knows what ice is for, and it melts. ② He tries straw, chaff and coal dust, and it still melts. ③ Calcutta, 16,000 miles away: the hatch opens and the ice is still there.
- **Payoff:** *His secret was sawdust, the stuff sawmills threw away.*

## Open fact questions (with the fact-checker)
Exact Gazette wording and date · 1806 cargo (80 vs 130 tons) · how much ice was shipped and how much survived the Calcutta trip (1833, ship *Tuscany*) · when sawdust became standard · leave out the debtors'-prison years.
