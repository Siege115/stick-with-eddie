# Tropical Harbour

**Status:** Built (look-dev), round 0 board `deliver/s02-round0-sets-part2.jpg`  ·  **Era:** 1806  ·  **Lighting:** tropic (Martinique) / tropicHaze (Calcutta)

**Contents:** One base (stone quay at y 0, water at y -2, the ship moored alongside with a gangplank) with two dressings. **Martinique 1806** (`place: martinique`): cream and white plaster with ochre trim, red clay tile roofs, blue and sea-green louvred shutters, street arcade with balconies of turned balusters, red-white striped awnings, fruit stalls (bananas, coconuts, pineapples, breadfruit, a cup of water), coconut palms and a breadfruit tree, rowing boats and a canoe, turquoise water, Mont Pelée backdrop; signs in plain French readable in English (CAFÉ, FRUITS, PORT). **Calcutta 1833** (`place: calcutta`): pale yellow-ochre stucco, flat roofs with balustrade parapets, two-storey colonnades and deep verandahs with green louvres and chick blinds, plain cloth canopies on bamboo poles, cloth bales, brass and clay water pots, a wide ghat down to brown-green river water, a banyan with aerial roots, country boats and a budgerow, a palanquin at rest, neoclassical skyline with a spire and the Ochterlony column; English signs (CUSTOM HOUSE, STRAND ROAD, CLOTH MERCHANT).

**Log cameo spot:** the Martinique set has an open upper window with a lit room behind it (`cameoSpot` in the set handle) for the one-frame log cameo.
