# Lessons Learned

- **Show stills before the video.** The owner reviews pictures first; it saves 45-minute renders.
- Unquoted YAML descriptions containing colons break parsing, so always quote them.
- Long background tasks must be resumable (container restarts happen).
- Builds made from overlapping shapes leave inner outlines (the "bump"). Use one smooth silhouette.
- Never let props cross characters; screens and walls must exist *before* people walk behind them.
- Synth notes must fade out, or the music sounds glitchy and 8-bit.
- The music style is warm, acoustic-sounding pizzicato and piano, never chiptune.
- Text on props must be English and spelled out for kids ("MONDAY", "FREE ADMISSION").
