# Animation Rules

- No pose held longer than 2 s (auto-variation every 1.6 s).
- Every gesture points at or does something; no arm-waving.
- Walking: feet plant, stride matches distance. Experts march in two lines.
- Counts on screen = clops = numbers said (S01: 21 taps).
- Nothing passes through walls or props; people walk *behind* things.
