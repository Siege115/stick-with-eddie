# Camera and Transitions

- A new angle or place every 2–4 s: low angles, over-the-shoulder, extreme close-ups, wide establishing shots, a foreground object near the lens, quick push-ins.
- The hero object gets its own close-up.
- Transitions are camera moves: whip pan, zoom in/out, push through a door, pull back, match cut. Hard cuts only where planned.
