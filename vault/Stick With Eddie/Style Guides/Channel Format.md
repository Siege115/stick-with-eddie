# Channel Format

**Script shape (~65 s, ~170–190 words):**
1. **0–4 s hook:** a concrete action by "he" or "she", no setup.
2. **"You see…"**, then the situation in 2–3 lines.
3. **Conflict:** someone powerful laughs or doubts.
4. **Escalation in threes:** once, nothing; again, they laugh harder; then it happens for real.
5. **One line of dialogue** in a character's voice, for humour.
6. **Payoff in the last sentence**, which also explains how it works. Ends cold: no "subscribe", no outro. The last frame loops into the first.

Plain words, short sentences, a new beat every 2–4 s. Titles: 4–7 words, a curiosity gap, no clickbait punctuation. Facts must be true, with sources in the description.
