# Visual Style

- **Characters:** flat cut-outs, thick black outlines, round white heads (Eddie: egg-shaped, bald), **pill-shaped coloured bodies**, thin stick arms and legs, dot eyes with expressive brows. Costumes carry the story. Heads stay flat white (soft rim light only).
- **Contact shadow** under every character and hoof; long sun shadows in warm scenes.
- **Worlds:** full 3D sets with perspective, textured floors and walls, signage, coloured lighting per scene (warm / cool / spot), fog and depth.
- **Detail:** rich background dressing (shutters, curtains, plants, posters, pipes, props); hats with sheen and bows, beard strands, glasses glints.
- **Text** on props in English, for this audience.
