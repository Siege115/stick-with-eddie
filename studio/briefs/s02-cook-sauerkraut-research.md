# s02-cook-sauerkraut: research note (candidate for Short #2, alternate 1)

Status: idea stage, waiting for the owner's pick. Not fact-checked yet. Research: free web search, 2026-11-17.

## The true story
- **1768:** Lt. **James Cook** sails HMS *Endeavour* from Plymouth on a voyage of almost 3 years. On long voyages, **scurvy** (from lack of vitamin C) regularly crippled crews.
- The ship carries about **7,860 lb of "sour krout"** (one source says 3 tons) as an anti-scurvy food, alongside malt and other items.
- The sailors refuse to eat the sauerkraut.
- Cook's own journal, 13 Apr 1769: *"The Sour Krout the Men at first would not eat until I put in practice a Method I never once knew to fail with seamen, and this was to have some of it dress'd every Day for the Cabbin Table, and permitted all the Officers without exception to make use of it, and left it to the option of the Men either to take as much as they pleased or none at all; but this practice was not continued above a week before I found it necessary to put every one on board to an Allowance…"* He adds that if something comes from the officers, the men think it is the finest stuff in the world. The exact wording and date need checking against the SL NSW transcription.
- **Outcome:** scurvy was rare on board. Contemporary records suggest **nobody died of it**, though one source counts up to three possibly scurvy-related deaths. **Wording must be careful:** "almost no scurvy", not "nobody got sick". Sauerkraut is only a modest source of vitamin C, so don't claim it was a miracle cure. The story is about the trick.

## Hidden reason / payoff
Cook knew the sailors would refuse anything they were *ordered* to eat, but they'd want anything the *officers* were eating. Reverse psychology turned cabbage into a luxury.

## Story shape (draft)
- **Working title:** The Captain's Cabbage Trick
- **Hook:** "He served the cabbage only to the officers, and he did it on purpose."
- **"You see…"** In 1768 sailors on long voyages got scurvy: their gums bled, they got weak. Cook had barrels of pickled cabbage on board, and the crew wouldn't touch it.
- **Conflict:** the sailors grumble and laugh, and one tips his bowl overboard. "Captain's rotten cabbage!"
- **Rule of three:**
  1. Day one: cabbage on the officers' table only. The sailors laugh.
  2. A few days later: the sailors peek through the cabin window. The officers are enjoying it.
  3. Within a week the sailors are demanding it, and Cook has to *ration* the cabbage.
- **Payoff:** "Sailors refuse what they're told to eat, but they'll fight for what the officers get. And Cook's crew stayed almost free of scurvy."
- Sensitivity: keep the story on the ship and about the food. Cook's later landings in the Pacific are a sensitive colonial history and are not part of this Short.
